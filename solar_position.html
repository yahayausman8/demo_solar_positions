Zenith (Z) is measured from straight overhead; elevation (α) from the horizon, with α = 90° − Z. When the sun drops below the horizon, α < 0° and Z > 90° — that is night. Elevation and zenith are computed from latitude, day of year, and solar time. Horizontal placement is schematic (morning at left, afternoon at right).
